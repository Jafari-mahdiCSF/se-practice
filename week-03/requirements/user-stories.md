# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

Six reviewed stories are retained. Roles are limited to Student and Administrator.

The template placeholders have been replaced with reviewed stories.

---

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view available rooms and time slots, so that I can choose a suitable study period.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability reflects current bookings and room status when the student searches.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book an available room for a future time slot, so that I can reserve a place to study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A student may create a booking only for a room and time slot shown as available.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that the room becomes available for others.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only reservations belonging to that student.

### US-04
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that I can keep room availability accurate.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** An administrator can change the service status of any study room.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a selected period, so that I can understand how rooms are being used.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Usage information is available for completed and current bookings in the selected period.

### US-06
**Story:** As a Student, I want the system to confirm my booking or cancellation, so that I know the requested change was recorded.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A confirmation is produced after a booking or cancellation succeeds.
